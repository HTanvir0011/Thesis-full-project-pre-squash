# Statistical analysis plan (frozen with freeze.json before any locked-set evaluation)

Version 1, 2026-10-27. Anything not listed here is exploratory and labelled as such in the thesis/paper.

## Data discipline
- CT split: patient-level, hash(seed, patient) -> 70/10/20 train/val/test (`tools/make_split.py`; file hash in freeze.json). Usable-CT rules are in that script.
- Locked real sets (never used for any decision, training, tuning or model selection): SZCH test (75 DES pairs), JSRT, Node21, TBX11K validation, VinDr-RibCXR.
- Development real sets (may inform pilots but not final selection): NIH validation part, VinBigData validation part.
- Model selection (which arm is "ours"): validation CTs only, by the rule in H1. Real-image results never choose a model.
- Hyper-parameters are identical across arms (resolution, optimiser, schedule, batch, EMA, augmentation strength) except the one flag that defines the arm.

## Hypotheses and primary endpoints
- **H1 (synthetic test CTs, exact truth).** Endpoint: lesion absorption A from the insertion audit (lesions from other test CTs; 4 sites per CT).
  Contrast of each lesion-aware arm (`nodule_loss`, `clia`, `clia_nodule`) vs `recipe`: per-CT mean of A, paired, patient-cluster bootstrap 95% CI,
  Holm over the 3 contrasts. Co-primary non-inferiority: per-CT bone-image MAE, margin = 5% of the `recipe` mean MAE (one-sided 5%, `stats.non_inferior`).
  Pass: A lower for at least one lesion-aware arm (Holm p < 0.05) AND non-inferior MAE.
- **H2 (real paired, SZCH n=75).** Endpoint: bone-region PSNR_cal. Selected-ours vs Qure released weights: non-inferiority margin 0.5 dB
  (and 0.01 for SSIM_cal); superiority tested separately (paired Wilcoxon, BCa/percentile bootstrap CI). Identity and CLAHE are reference controls.
- **H3 (real lesion audit).** Endpoint: A at alpha=1, 6 sites x 75 images, site-level paired differences (ours - Qure) with image-cluster bootstrap CI.
  Interaction: difference in (ours - Qure) between the highest and lowest bone-overlap tertile (overlap measured on the DES bone signal).
  Reported next to suppression gain (H2) because A=0 is trivially reached by doing nothing.
- **H4 (downstream).** Per dataset, input variants {orig, soft, both}: NIH macro-AUROC (DeLong per class, Holm over classes; patient-cluster bootstrap for macro);
  Node21/TBX11K/VinBig detection: FROC CPM (IoU 0.3) and mAP@50 with image-level bootstrap; 3 seeds, mean +- SD, seed as random effect.
  Subgroup: recall at 2 FP/image for lesions with >= 15% bone overlap vs < 15%; difference-in-differences of (soft - orig) with bootstrap CI.
- **H5 (reliability).** Spearman between ensemble disagreement and absolute bone error on SZCH; conformal coverage on held-out synthetic CTs (nominal 90%); real-shift coverage reported as measured.

## Multiplicity
Holm within each hypothesis family. Secondary/exploratory metrics are reported with unadjusted CIs and labelled as such.
